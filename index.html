How Rialo PM Works

01

First Round

Begin with the 36-team single league standings. Predict the top-performing teams, sort their positions, and lock in your choices.

02

Generate Bracket

Once selections are locked, the system automatically builds the knockout bracket, mapping out the playoffs and the path to the round of 16.

03

Pick Winners

Advance through the rounds by clicking your match winners all the way to the final. Your bracket updates instantly with every pick.

04

RLO Entry & Submit

Input your RLO stake, review your potential returns, and finalize your entry once all league standings and tournament picks are set.

05

Prize Pool Access

Entering your Champions League winner prediction automatically qualifies you for the 40,000 RLO Prize Pool, distributed right after the grand final on 5 June 2027.

06

Smart Winning System

The winning system does not require your predictions to be 100% correct. Rewards are based on how close your bracket predictions are to the real results. For example, if you correctly predict one team in the quarter-finals, you will receive a certain share. If you correctly predict three teams, your reward share increases, and so on. The closer your predictions are, the higher your potential reward.

Higher-risk champion picks can increase your odds. Favorites are safer but give lower returns.
